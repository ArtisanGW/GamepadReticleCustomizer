**Summary:** Changes the controller's reticle: its shape, size and color, or an icon on your target.
**Categories:** Combat, Artwork

## Description

With the controller UI on, the game shows a small white dot as its reticle. This addon lets you change how it looks, and turn it into an icon on your target.

[![Support me on Ko-fi](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=storage.ko-fi.com/cdn/kofi5.png&h=36)](https://ko-fi.com/artisangw)

It's a simple tool with no dependencies:
- Shape, size, color and opacity, with a color picker that works on a controller
- A border around it, in its own color and size: an outline, so open shapes like a cross get it on every side
- A preview on the settings page
- Settings in Options > AddOns > Gamepad Reticle Customizer, or /gprc

It starts as the game's own dot, so nothing changes until you choose.

## On a target

A choice for each kind of target, each starting at None (the reticle stays):
- **Horde and Alliance players** (yours first): their faction badge or their class icon, each faction with its own class icon style, so friend and foe are easy to tell apart
- **Friendly NPCs**: their job, with the game's own icon (vendor, trainer, quest, flight master…)
- **Neutral NPCs**: the game's gray dots, a yellow paw, the game's neutral, Alliance or Horde shield, its PvP badge, a sword or the War Mode swords
- **Enemy NPCs**: a sword, the game's attack cursor, or the War Mode swords
- Any of them: a raid marker or one of the game's icons

The sword, the paw, the shields, the PvP badge and the War Mode swords are gray while none of your action bar spells can reach the target, and in color once one can.

Icons show while the reticle sits on your target's nameplate. The game keeps the reticle in the middle of the screen, so use [Gamepad Reticle On Target](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1819101) to put it there.

More shapes and class icon styles come with [Jiberish Fabled Icons](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/jiberish-fabled-icons), if you have it installed. Its art stays its own: this addon only shows it, and never needs it.

## Goes well with
- [Gamepad Fast Map](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1827949): opens and closes the world map without its hitch.
- [Gamepad Reticle On Target](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1819101): puts the reticle on your target, so it stays on what you attack.
- [Gamepad Camera Offset](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1818297): moves the camera over your shoulder.
