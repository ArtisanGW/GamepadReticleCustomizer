# Gamepad Reticle Customizer

## 1.0.0

First release.

- The controller's reticle: its shape, size, color, opacity and border
- A color picker that works on a controller
- An icon on your target while the reticle sits on it: a player's class, a raid marker or one of the game's icons
- More shapes and class icon styles with Jiberish Fabled Icons, when installed
- Starts as the game's own dot: nothing changes until you choose
- Settings in Options > AddOns > Gamepad Reticle Customizer, or /grc
