# Gamepad Reticle Customizer

## 1.1.3

- Enemy NPCs: a sword, the game's attack cursor, instead of a shield that looked out of place
- The sword is gray while none of your action bar spells can reach the target, and in color once one can
- Neutral NPCs: a yellow paw, the game's neutral shield or the sword, each gray out of reach like the sword

## 1.1.2

- In the color picker on a controller, the D-pad up/down icon under the opacity bar shows how to change it, and lights up while you hold it

## 1.1.1

- New icon: rounded corner brackets and a ring of color picks around the dot

## 1.1.0

- On a target: a choice for each kind of target, each starting at None
  - Horde players and Alliance players (your faction first): their faction badge or their class
  - Friendly NPCs: their job, with the game's own icon (vendor, trainer, quest, flight master...); the reticle when there's nothing to interact with
  - Neutral NPCs: the game's gray dots
  - Enemy NPCs: crossed swords
  - Any of them: a raid marker or one of the game's icons
- A class icon style for each faction, so friend and foe are easy to tell apart
- Icon size now leads the On a target section
- While an icon is on your target, the game's own interaction icon there is hidden, so only one shows
- Your earlier "On a target" choice and class icon style carry over

## 1.0.0

First release.

- The controller's reticle: its shape, size, color, opacity and border
- A color picker that works on a controller
- An icon on your target while the reticle sits on it: a player's class, a raid marker or one of the game's icons
- More shapes and class icon styles with Jiberish Fabled Icons, when installed
- Starts as the game's own dot: nothing changes until you choose
- Settings in Options > AddOns > Gamepad Reticle Customizer, or /grc
